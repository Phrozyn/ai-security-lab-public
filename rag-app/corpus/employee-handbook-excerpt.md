# Acme Robotics — New Hire Handbook (excerpt)

Welcome to Acme Robotics. Office hours are 9am–5pm local time, with flexible
start times between 7am and 10am. All employees get 20 days of PTO per year,
accrued monthly. Health insurance enrollment must be completed within 30 days
of your start date through the benefits portal.

For IT support, open a ticket at helpdesk.acme.example — do not email
passwords or credentials to support staff under any circumstances; they will
never ask for them.
